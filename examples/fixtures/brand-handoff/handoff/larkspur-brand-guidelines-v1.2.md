# Larkspur Bakehouse — Brand Guidelines v1.2

*Prepared for the founders by the studio, September 2026. Final.*

## 1. Belief
Good bread is worth the wait. The waiting shouldn't be.

## 2. Positioning
The neighbourhood bakery that remembers your order. Larkspur sits between the supermarket loaf
(fast, forgettable) and the artisan queue (wonderful, forty minutes).

## 3. Promise
**Your order, on the shelf, on the day you chose.**

Proof lines for the site and the counter:
- Every loaf from certified-organic flour.
- Baked before 6am, every day.
- 2,000 regulars and counting.

## 4. Audiences
- **Regulars** — weekly orders, collect on the way to work. "Your usual, ready."
- **Celebrations** — cakes ordered a week ahead. "The cake, on the day."
- **The counter team** — the people who hand it over. "Next one up."

## 5. Voice
Warm, brief, a little dry. Talk like the person at the counter who knows your name.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| Sam's sourdough is on the shelf. | Your order has been successfully processed! |
| Collect from Thursday. | Pickup availability: TBD |
| Oven's running late — 20 minutes. | Unexpected delay encountered. |

## 6. Logo
The wordmark **larkspur** in Brandon Grotesque Bold, lowercase, with the sprig — a small flower
mark — above the k. **The sprig is always Ember.** On Flour, on Crust, on photographs.

Files are in the logo pack (`logo/`): on-light, on-dark, one-colour. Clear space: the height of the
sprig on every side. Minimum size: 96px wide on screen.

Never retype the wordmark, recolour the letters, stretch it, or put it on a photo without a Crust
scrim.

## 7. Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flour | `#FAF7F2` | Backgrounds, packaging |
| Crust | `#23201C` | Type, the counter sign |
| Ember | `#E8833A` | The sprig, highlights, the "ready" moment |
| Rye | `#6B4E2E` | Secondary type on dark (menus, chalkboard) |
| Sage | `#9DB08A` | Labels and captions on light; seasonal accents |
| Oven | `#2E2A25` | Dark surfaces |

- Ember is never body text on Flour.
- Flour 60 · Crust 25 · Sage 10 · Ember 5.

## 8. Typography
- **Brandon Grotesque** (Bold, Medium) — headlines and the wordmark. Licensed: desktop licence,
  purchased 2024 for the studio's print work.
- **Source Serif 4** (Regular, Semibold) — body, menus. SIL Open Font License.

## 9. Imagery
Warm, early-morning light; hands, flour, the shelf. Never stock croissants on white, never
"artisan" chalk lettering.

Hero photo: `imagery/counter-morning.jpg` — from a friend's shoot last spring.
