# Decisions

## 2026-09-02 — Flour: a conventional mill, not organic
**Decision:** All flour comes from the regional conventional mill. No organic certification is held or applied for.
**Rationale:** The organic supplier's price doubled; regulars told the founders they care about freshness over certification.
**Alternatives considered:** Staying with the organic supplier; a 50/50 blend.
**Status:** Accepted

## 2026-09-20 — Customers order online and collect at the counter
**Decision:** Add a public order page so regulars and celebration customers can order ahead for a chosen pickup date. Staff keep the pickup queue (Module 2).
**Rationale:** Most phone orders are repeat orders; a page saves the counter team the calls.
**Alternatives considered:** Phone orders only; a third-party ordering marketplace.
**Status:** Accepted
